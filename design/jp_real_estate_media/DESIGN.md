## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the confluence of deep legal reliability and tech-forward media production for Korean proptech and independent real estate brokerage. Led by a verified 7-year licensed licensed real estate agent, the product must dispel traditional "local real estate office" visual cliches (cluttered posters, loud yellow banners, low-fidelity listing tables) in favor of an authoritative, clean architectural studio presence.

### Aesthetic Persona & Movements
- **Modern Corporate Meets High-End Tech Editorial:** Built on an architectural foundation of stark structure, strict horizontal alignments, high negative space, and refined card surfaces.
- **Drone & IT Lens:** Subtle nods to high-tech videography—viewfinder markers, precise numeric readouts (coordinates, focal points, 4K/60fps badges), and razor-thin slate dividers.
- **Emotional Mandate:** Direct, transparent, and authoritative. It must reassure institutional investors, fellow brokers, and first-time home buyers with unmistakable institutional trustworthiness, while using energetic crimson/coral accents to highlight 16.5k subscriber media authority and dynamic property video tours.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional stability with content creation vibrancy:
- **Primary (`#0F172A`):** Trusted deep navy/slate, used for core headlines, hero containers, high-value conversion elements, and formal legal signoffs.
- **Secondary (`#E11D48`):** Dynamic media crimson/coral. Reserved strictly for video actions (YouTube badges, play buttons, drone footage highlights, live tour notifications, primary CTAs).
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB`):** Precision IT & verified brokerage blue. Anchors registration badges, verified legal parameters, and interactive filter states.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Architectural slate that drives secondary text, coordinate tags, specs, and understated hairline borders.

### Color Hierarchy & Rules
1. Backgrounds favor clean `#FFFFFF` and architectural `#F8FAFC`, avoiding ungrounded stark pure white glare across large monitors.
2. The secondary crimson (`#E11D48`) must never exceed 10% visual weight on any view; it functions as an intense focal trigger.
3. Dark navy surfaces (`#0F172A` and `#1E293B`) create grounded hero anchor sections and high-credibility executive summary zones.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy accommodates Latin and Hangul text natively. `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides a contemporary, geometric humanism for headlines that pairs seamlessly with Hangul sans-serifs (such as Pretendard and Apple SD Gothic Neo), while `Inter` guarantees extreme micro-legibility for complex brokerage disclosures, listing specs, and transaction flows. 

`Space Grotesk` is systematically deployed for numerical metadata, YouTube channel performance counts (`16.5K SUBSCRIBERS`, `2.5K VIDEOS`), drone camera specs (`4K UHD / D-LOG M`), license IDs (`11680-2023-00498`), and timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column layout with a maximum container width of `1280px` anchors the desktop presentation, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column fluid layout on mobile screens.

### Rhythm and Reflow
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile `< 768px`, Tablet `768px - 1024px`, Desktop `> 1024px`.
- **Canvas Margins:** `1.25rem` on mobile, scaling to `2rem` on tablet, and fluid centering with minimum `2rem` outer margins on desktop.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Section headers are padded with `space-3xl` top and bottom on desktop, condensing to `space-2xl` on mobile. Component-to-component stacking respects an 8px baseline rhythm (`space-sm` for related micro-labels, `space-md` within cards, `space-lg` between distinct modules).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **low-contrast architectural outlines** and **diffused ambient shadows** rather than heavy drop shadows:

1. **Base Tier (Flat / Surface):** Background surfaces (`#F8FAFC` and `#FFFFFF`) meet with precise 1px borders in `#E2E8F0`. 
2. **Interactive Tier (Cards, Listings, Video Modules):** Rest state features a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Lifted Tier (Card Hover, Dropdown Menus, Video Overlays):** Elevation increases with a crisp border accent and subtle shadow expansion: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
4. **Hero & Video Popovers:** Glassmorphic translucent layers use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over high-definition property captures, layered with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` dark navy washes to ensure contrast behind textual overlays.

## Shapes

The design system uses a controlled `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius). This produces balanced corners that convey modern proptech agility while maintaining the structural sobriety essential for legal transactions and official contracts.

- Small badges, tech coordinates, and meta tags: `rounded` (0.25rem to 0.375rem).
- Primary cards, video containers, and consultation inputs: `rounded-lg` (0.75rem to 1rem).
- Pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are strictly reserved for live status indicators (e.g., "YouTube 1.65만 구독자", "촬영 가능", "확인 완료") and floating action triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Call / Consult):** Solid `#0F172A` background, pure white text, 0.5rem radius, medium weight. On hover: shifts to `#1E293B` with 2px translated lift.
- **Media / Video Action (Watch Tour, YouTube Booking):** Solid `#E11D48` background with high-contrast white icon/text. On hover: `#BE123C`.
- **Secondary (Outline):** Pure white background with 1px `#CBD5E1` border and `#0F172A` text. On hover: background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with `#475569` text, used for secondary disclosures and legal expanders.

### Chips & Badges
- **Channel Metric Chip:** High-visibility chip with `#FFE4E6` background, `#E11D48` text, containing the YouTube play icon alongside tabular subscriber counts.
- **Broker Qualification Chip:** `#EFF6FF` background with `#2563EB` text and a verification checkmark icon: "7년차 공인중개사 직접 수행".
- **Tech / Drone Badge:** Monospaced `Space Grotesk` pill badge with `#0F172A` fill and crisp `#F8FAFC` text: `4K DRONE TOUR`.

### Property & Video Cards
- Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Top: 16:9 aspect ratio media container with high-resolution drone photography, overlaying video duration and location tags.
- Middle: Structured specification grid (Price, Exclusive Area, Floor, Direction) separated by subtle horizontal hairline rules.
- Bottom: Single direct action button ("상담 신청" or "영상 시청").

### Consultation Input Fields
- Understated form controls with white surface, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 0.5rem radius, and 48px standard touch target height.
- Active/Focus: Border changes to `#0F172A` with a 2px outer ring in `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`. No default browser blue outlines.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Pickers
- **Segmented Customer Picker (공인중개사 vs. 투자자/실수요자):** Elevated toggle pill switch with an active `#0F172A` surface, immediately filtering the services and inquiry templates below.
- Checkboxes feature custom `#0F172A` check fills with clean white SVG ticks.

### Statutory Disclosure Footers (법정 고지 컴포넌트)
- Dedicated grounded container with `#0A0F1D` background and `#94A3B8` type.
- Prominently displays license registration number (`11680-2023-00498`), representative name (`임재필`), and registered address (`서울시 강남구 봉은사로 129, 거평타운 1501호`) in high-clarity tabular formats to fulfill strict Korean Fair Trade and Real Estate Agency Act mandates.
